How to play
  • 36 cards: three each of 1 to 12. Every hand is sorted low to high, and only its two ends can be reached.
  • On your turn, reveal cards one at a time: flip any middle card, or ask any player for their lowest or highest card. You can ask yourself. Ask the same end again and you get the next card in.
  • Same number as before? Keep going. Different number? Everything flips back and your turn ends. Remember what you saw.
  • Three of a kind and you take the trio. The turn passes.
  • Simple: first to three trios wins. Spicy: you need two linked trios instead; linked numbers add up to 7 or differ by 7, and are printed small on each card. In both modes, the trio of 7s wins on the spot.
  • Pencil notes mark every face-down card you have already seen. Leave it off for the real memory game.
